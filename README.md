### *Neighborhood Canopy Registry & Urban Carbon Offset Estimator*

*An open-source geospatial tool for communities to map local tree canopy cover, track specimen health, and estimate environmental carbon offsets with an immersive deep-forest canopy UI.*

</div>

---

## 📖 Features
* **🗺️ Interactive Canopy Map:** Dual-layer OpenStreetMap & Google Satellite GIS viewer.
* **📍 Map Click-to-Pin:** Click on map points to auto-populate exact latitude/longitude coordinates.
* **🍃 Carbon Sequestration Estimator:** Uses standardized growth formulas to estimate annual $\text{CO}_2$ offset.
* **📊 Urban Forestry Analytics:** Interactive Plotly charts mapping species diversity and carbon impact.
